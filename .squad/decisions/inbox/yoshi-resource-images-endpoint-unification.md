# Resource Images: unify the 24 per-domain image endpoints; merge the two owner enums into `DomainOwnerType`; close the flat sub-resources

**What:** Four decisions settled by Skyler, 2026-10-12, on `docs/resource-images/endpoint-unification.md` (Phase 7 / F1 of the custom-moves plan).

**(1) Unify the image endpoints — Q1(A).** The twenty-four image actions duplicated across `MonstersController`, `MinionsController`, `LocationsController` and `BystandersController` become one `ResourceImagesController` with six actions behind an anchored, case-insensitive `{owner:regex(^(monsters|minions|locations|bystanders)$)}` segment — the `CustomMovesController` mechanism copied exactly. The URLs do not change, so there is no schema change, no migration, no image-service logic change and no frontend change beyond one stale comment. Error mapping adopts `ToErrorResult` (Q4), verified to change no response today. Swagger's loss is accepted without an `IOperationFilter` (Q5); the threshold for revisiting is a third parameterised-owner route. `design.md` §3's *"not a new fifth cross-cutting controller"* sentence is superseded in writing, struck through in place rather than rewritten, since that document is a closed record.

**(2) Merge the two owner enums into one five-member `DomainOwnerType` — Q3(B), against my recommendation.** This supersedes `docs/domain-custom-moves/architecture.md` §2.8, seven days after it shipped; that section is left intact with an amendment note, because its central fact is still true and is now what the replacement must encode explicitly. The merge lands as its own **first** commit (U0), never folded into the switchover: it is a 368-token, 41-file, compiler-proven rename across shipped green code, and burying a 24-action deletion inside it would make the one commit that must be read line by line unreadable. Consequences, worked: the three `_ => throw` arms in `ResourceImageService` become genuinely reachable, but `OwnerExistsAsync` dominates the other two in every call path that exists, so the blast radius is one method; the throws **stay** rather than becoming `Mystery => false` arms, because a false arm would compile and pass silently in a world where mystery images *had* been built, and because "make it unrepresentable" collapses back into the declined two-enum design; the route stays at **four** segments (Q2), with the feature boundary carried by a declared `IResourceImageService.ImageOwnerTypes` set that the route alternation, the controller dictionary and a bidirectional drift test all read; TypeScript gets `ImageOwnerType = Exclude<DomainOwnerType, 'Mystery'>`, which is **strictly better** than today's two hand-written unions; and it is **not a migration at all**, because `MotwDbContextModelSnapshot` records the provider type (`b.Property<string>("OwnerType")`) and not the CLR type, so merging now with rows present is exactly as safe as merging later. The five member names are a **data contract** under `HasConversion<string>()` and must not change.

**(3) The flat sub-resources are considered and declined — Q7, "images only, close the question."** Powers, weaknesses, armors and attacks stay duplicated across Monster and Minion. Not deferred, not parked. **This does not close `docs/domain-custom-moves/architecture.md` §12 F2**, which is the create-page batch primitive and was not among that question's options; the two have both been called "F2" across a revision boundary and the record now says which is which.

**(4) Four commits, in order: U0 enum merge / U1 the four-owner endpoint oracle (test-only) / U2 the atomic switchover / U3 the drift guard and the record.**

**Why:** The image endpoints' HTTP surface is the only layer that was built four times — the table, the service and the Angular client are all already unified — so unification removes 332 lines of fourfold-copied controller code, seven duplications with no guard of any kind (including four copies each of `[RequestSizeLimit]` and the 24 MiB constant), and a coverage hole where three of four domains have **no** automated HTTP test, justified today only by a comment asserting the four controllers are identical wrappers. Three of the four reasons Phase 7 gave for this being harder than custom moves did not survive measurement: the actions are non-uniform between the six verbs, not between the four copies; the "live consumers" objection *inverts*, because deadness is what let custom moves change a contract and nothing here changes one; and the enum convergence does not happen on an enum edit. On Q3 specifically, I recommended keeping two enums and was overruled; working the reversal through found nothing that makes it unwise, one place where it is strictly better than what I proposed (the TypeScript `Exclude<>` subset, replacing two unrelated hand-written lists with a derived one), and exactly one real cost — the image-owner set stops being derivable from `Enum.GetValues` and becomes a transcribed four-element constant with a drift test over it. The highest-value new risk it creates is that merging the TypeScript unions *without* the `Exclude<>` narrowing silently forces a dead `Mystery: 'mysteries'` key into the images segment map, which `SUPPRESS_ERROR_TOAST` hides and which the one existing cross-guard passes over.
