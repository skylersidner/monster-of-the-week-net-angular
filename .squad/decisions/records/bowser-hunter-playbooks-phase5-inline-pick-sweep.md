### Hunter Playbooks — Phase 5 swept for inline picks the old tooling could not see; 14 candidates, 10 high-confidence, across 5 playbooks (2026-08-30)
**By:** Bowser (Backend Developer / DevOps)
**What:** Per Skyler's call ("Sweep Phase 5 with the new tooling"), re-ran detection across all 28 playbooks' bespoke content with the inline option-run path built in the previous pass. New script `tools/pdf-extract/sweep-inline-picks.mjs` scans **all 58 pages in full** — columns detected geometrically per page, not limited to the regions prior build scripts happened to target — and runs the *same* detector `extract-moves.mjs` uses (factored into `lib-move-body.mjs` and imported by both, so the sweep cannot drift from the validated extractor). Output: `phase5-inline-pick-sweep.json`/`.html`. **Detection and reporting only — nothing under `docs/hunter-playbooks/` was read from or written to; `bespoke-ruleset-catalogue.md` is untouched and every hit comes from the source PDF. Yoshi adjudicates each one.** Result: **123 hits — 14 Phase 5 bespoke-scope candidates (10 high confidence, 12 creation-time, 2 in-play), 16 Moves-scope, 93 standard sheet sections.** The high-confidence creation-time worklist spans **five playbooks**: **The Crooked** p12 Underworld (four separate nested picks — "Pick one: vampire, werewolf, troll, reptiloid", "…sorcerer, demon, faerie, psychic", "…immortal, god, outsider, witch", "…(choose one): a horde of goblins, a hunger of ghouls, a dream-eater, a salamander"); **The Chosen** p7 Your Special Weapon's third part ("Finally, pick what material the business-end is made from: add "steel," "cold iron," … or anything else you want" — 9 options; its Form and Business-end parts are bulleted and were authored, only Material is inline); **The Monstrous** p37 Pure Drive (the original find, 11 options); **The Snoop** p48 Crew ("Crew jobs: camera, sound, editing, dogsbody, researcher, driver, director, producer, bodyguard" — 9); and **The Visitor** p56 Expatriation (three bullet lines, 6–7 culture descriptors each, 20 options total).

**Why:** Two things make this more than a list. First, **the sweep's recall was cross-checked independently rather than asserted** — a separate `pdftotext -raw` regex grep produced 98 pattern hits, all of which the sweep accounts for, and that check exposed two genuine coverage gaps in the sweep's first version which were fixed before shipping rather than documented as limitations: an inline run living inside a `<li>` (The Curse-eater's Ropes of Fate) and, more importantly, **a colon-bearing instruction in prose with the option runs in the bullet lines below it — which is exactly what The Visitor's Expatriation is**, and it would otherwise have been missed entirely. Expatriation is the very section `custom-moves-ideation.md` §3.1 cites as the `MinSelect < MaxSelect` precedent, so its counts are modeled while its 20 options may not be. Second, **the report distinguishes real picks from noise along two independent axes**, both reported per hit so the filtering is auditable: confidence (does a pick instruction actually govern the run, checked against the surrounding sentence rather than the clause before the colon; plus option shape), and **timing** — a roll-outcome or hold-spend framing means the list is chosen fresh each time in play, which Q1 already settled as prose. The Crooked's Burglar and Fixer both parse like textbook creation-time picks until you read the sentence around them; they are flagged and downgraded, not dropped, so the call stays Yoshi's. Three further things Yoshi should know: **The Crooked is a Phase 4 pilot playbook** and carries four of the ten high-confidence hits, all nested one level inside Underworld's own options — a second, independent cause of the sequencing risk the Phase 6 census already raised about Crooked; **"Look, pick one from each list:" is a real inline comma-list on all 28 playbooks** but is deliberately in the standard-sections bucket rather than the candidate list, called out explicitly so its absence reads as a decision and not a miss (if Look is not modeled elsewhere, that bucket is where to look); and the Moves-scope and standard buckets are kept in the report rather than discarded precisely so the scope filter can be checked rather than trusted. Full method, the two column-attribution bugs found and fixed along the way, and the complete findings table are in `docs/hunter-playbooks/pdf-extraction-pipeline.md`'s "Twentieth pass" section. All 38 previously-committed review outputs remain byte-identical to their git blobs after the shared-library refactor, and all 8 Phase 6 tooling targets still assert clean.
