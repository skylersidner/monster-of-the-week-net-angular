### 2026-08-31: Multi-Minion in the Mystery Wizard — Roster + Single Active Composer (UX design, advisory)
**By:** Rosalina (Designer)

**What:** Produced `docs/updates/multi-minion-wizard-design.md`, the UX/visual half of multi-minion support in the mystery-create wizard (Yoshi's `docs/updates/multi-minion-support.md` covers the state/data half). Recommended pattern: extend the locations/bystanders "summary list above, authoring form below" pattern the wizard already uses twice (`mystery-create-locations-phase.html:1-29`, `mystery-create-bystanders-phase.html:1-29`) with an edit affordance per row and an explicit `Save Minion` on the form — a **roster of minion summary cards** over **one composer** that is the only place the four sub-resource panels ever render. Roster card markup lifted verbatim from `minions-list.html:16-41` (name + `bg-badge-minion` type badge + `<small>` counts line + `shrink-0` `action-btn--edit`/`--delete` cluster with `app-icon` pencil/trash), which is already the shipped minion-card vocabulary on `monster-detail.html:29-37` too. Composer is open when the roster is empty (so a first-time user sees essentially today's step) and collapses to an outline-accent `+ Add Another Minion` otherwise. Dossier: one `Minions` section, one compact name+badge+counts entry per minion, with **exactly one expanded** — whichever is loaded in the composer.

**Why / judgment calls:**

1. **Height stays constant in N.** The whole point of roster+composer over an accordion is that only one minion's four sub-lists are ever rendered; three minions cost three ~72px rows, not three ~900px blocks. Rejected the accordion explicitly: at `w-[42%]` (`mystery-create.html:5`) the sub-resource add-forms are *already* wrapping (`flex-[1_1_220px]`, `flex: 0 1 160px`), so nesting an authoring form inside a list row costs another level of horizontal padding, and the ~72px→~900px expand/collapse delta scroll-jumps the panel. Also rejected modal-per-minion (breaks the live dossier preview, and this app's only modal is `ConfirmDeleteModalComponent`), tabs, and an inner master-detail (no room).
2. **`Save Minion` must be outline-accent, not filled.** `Next →` is `bg-accent text-on-accent` in the same 42% column (`mystery-create.html:36`); a second filled accent button one scroll away is a genuine hierarchy collision. Used the outline-accent treatment every `+ Add` button in the wizard already carries (`mystery-create-monster-phase.html:64`).
3. **Nesting fix:** composer takes the locations add-form's `border border-default rounded-lg p-4` wrapper, and the four inner panels drop from `border border-default` to borderless `bg-surface-sunken` — one step down the elevation ladder, correct in both themes. Deliberately makes the minion step's panels lighter than the monster step's *within the same phase*, justified by hierarchy (monster panels are top-level, minion panels are now nested one deep), not taste. Keeping the borders is an acceptable smaller v1 diff.
4. **Divergence from Yoshi's decision #10, flagged rather than accommodated.** He recommends deleting `minionSectionStarted`/`minionNameRequired`/`minionNameMissing` and dropping the `phase === 1 && step === 1` branch of `validateCurrentStep()` (`mystery-create.store.ts:913`) as dead code. Right for the roster, wrong for the composer: `Next` still has to tell an *empty* composer (proceed) from a *started-but-nameless* one (block), or a user who typed a description and added two attacks loses all of it silently. `minionNameRequired()` does die (Name gets an unconditional `*`); `minionSectionStarted()`'s detection survives, repurposed. Single governing rule for the whole step: **an open composer is committed if it can be, and you're stopped if it can't** — which also answers the "silent data loss on draft switch" risk Yoshi flagged for Luigi/me *without* the discard-confirmation modal he suggested (it would fire on the most common interaction in the step and train dismissal).
5. **Confirm-on-remove for a whole minion, plain `×` for its sub-items.** Diverges from locations/bystanders' instant `×`, justified because a minion is compound (takes up to four sub-lists with it) and, on revisit, maps to a real hard `DELETE /api/minions/{id}`. Consistent with the standing convention recorded in `luigi-hunter-playbooks-tracks-overflow-and-delete-confirm.md` that every destructive-delete surface goes through `ConfirmDeleteModalComponent`.
6. **Named the one real regression instead of hiding it:** the single-minion user — the common case — pays exactly one extra click (`Save Minion`) and one extra click to re-edit. Considered keeping the composer open in edit mode on the just-saved minion to avoid the collapse; rejected because it makes "did that save?" ambiguous.

**Stale premises corrected in Yoshi's plan (verified against current `main` `f872d5e`):** `DELETE /api/minions/{id}` now ships (`bowser-minion-delete-backend.md`), so MM-1 is done; a shared `MinionFormComponent` now exists (`features/minions/shared/minion-form/`), contradicting his decision #8, though it isn't directly reusable by the wizard (wrong lifecycle — own submit button, emits `UpsertMinionRequest`); and a complete standalone minion authoring page ships at `/monsters/:monsterId/minions/new` (`minion-create.html`), which is why "does the wizard still need inline sub-resource authoring at all" is now a live product question.

**Bug found:** `mystery-create-dossier.html:165` iterates `store.minionWeaknesses()` (raw live signal) inside a block otherwise reading `store.minionPreview()`. Harmless today (same array); under `minionDrafts` it becomes a visible cross-minion leak — collapsed minion #2 would render minion #1's weaknesses. Must be repointed when this lands.

**Confirmed no change needed:** `mystery-detail.html:95-113` already renders `minions()` as a list. Two consistency follow-ups flagged, neither blocking: it doesn't say which monster owns each minion (`monsterName` already exists on `MinionListItemResponse`, and `minions-list.html:23` already renders that exact line), and `monster-detail.html:19-41` has `+ Add Minion` but no remove affordance while `minions-list.html:32` does.

**Open for Skyler (7, in the doc):** most important is #1 — his phrase "add more than one Minion to an associated monster (and mystery)" is ambiguous, since `Minion.MonsterId` is a required non-nullable FK, so a minion can't attach to a mystery independent of a monster. Design assumes "N minions under the wizard's one monster." Also open: whether the wizard step still needs full sub-resource authoring now that `/monsters/:id/minions/new` exists, and whether the minion step's tracker dot should ever complete.

**Update 2026-09-01 — all seven open questions resolved by Skyler; design is Accepted.**
1. "N minions under the wizard's one monster" — not minions spanning several monsters. Assumption stood.
2. Keep full sub-resource authoring inline in the wizard's minion step.
3. Tracker dot lights up when `minionDrafts().length > 0` (replaces the hardcoded `false` at `mystery-create.store.ts:409`); step stays optional.
4. Always confirm on minion removal, via `ConfirmDeleteModalComponent` with `[items]` populated.
5. Add a "Belongs to {monster}" line to `mystery-detail`'s minion list.
6. **No** Remove Minion action on `monster-detail` — declined; the add-without-remove asymmetry stays open by decision.
7. **No** roster reordering — no ordering field, renders in creation order.

Also flagged to Skyler and still outstanding as implementation prerequisites: the `minionWeaknesses()`/`minionPreview()` mismatch at `mystery-create-dossier.html:165` should be fixed before drafts become a list, and `docs/updates/multi-minion-support.md` (Yoshi) needs a correction pass — three of its premises are stale, though its `minionDrafts` + `editingDraftIndex` state model survives intact.
