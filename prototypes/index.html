Mystery Creation Workflow

Three Design Concepts for Monster of the Week

Concept A: The Accumulating Dossier

A traditional split-screen layout with a horizontal progress tracker. As you work through the steps, the right panel builds a complete scrollable mystery dossier that accumulates all your entries in real-time.

View Prototype →

Concept B: The Focused Card Flip

A vertical stepper on the left guides your progress, while the right side features a sleek preview card that flips with a 3D animation whenever you move between phases, keeping focus on the current section.

View Prototype →

Concept C: The Keeper's Grimoire

An atmospheric dark-and-parchment split with a circular ritual ring tracker. The left panel resembles aged case files while the right features a terminal-style display with typing animations and collapsible sections.
